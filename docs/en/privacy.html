Your privacy

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: October 15, 2026
Operator: TidyHawk development team (GitHub @Lucky301)
Contact: support@supracore.co.kr

The current version of TidyHawk has no app account registration, advertising or tracking SDKs, or automatic transmission to a developer server. Cleanup and security checks run on this Mac.

1. Purpose and information handled

To provide its features, TidyHawk reads file names, paths, sizes, and timestamps within folders you authorize, installed apps and related files, running programs, and system security, account, and network information. Information it cannot access is marked as unavailable.

This information supports cleanup suggestions, identification of items used by running apps, security checks, and guidance. A security check does not confirm a malware infection or guarantee detection of every threat.

2. Information stored on this Mac

  • Folder access bookmarks and renewal history
  • Trust criteria for security items you have approved
  • The last 50 cleanup records, including dates, categories, successful and failed file paths, measured sizes, errors, and cancellation status

These are stored in the app’s local container. New cleanup records replace the oldest ones after 50 records. Other settings remain until you remove their stored data. Moving the app to Trash alone does not guarantee removal of its container data.

3. Support email and third parties

If you email support, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to respond to your request. We retain this information only as long as needed for support and remove it when no longer necessary, subject to any legal retention obligations. Privacy inquiries and deletion requests can be sent to support@supracore.co.kr.

The app does not automatically send files, scan results, or cleanup records to the developer or an AI service. Security reports are saved only to a location you select. Reports may include account names, paths, and settings; review them before sharing. Cloud storage or sharing services you choose have their own policies.

Opening official Apple help launches an Apple website in your browser. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub handles website visits under the GitHub privacy statement. This website contains no additional analytics or advertising scripts.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can revoke folder access in app settings and remove trust for individual security items. See the stored data guidance for local data removal. Revoking folder access does not erase existing cleanup records.

Cleaned files remain in Trash. Restore them using Finder. After Trash is emptied, normal Trash recovery is unavailable. TidyHawk does not empty Trash automatically.

5. Contact and changes

Contact the TidyHawk development team at support@supracore.co.kr. Include only the information needed to resolve your issue. Do not send passwords, personal files, or entire security reports.

When features or data handling change, we update this policy and its revision date, and update the app’s notice when necessary.
